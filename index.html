ABOUT US

Our Journey

Started in 2016 with 2 broadlooms, adding up to 10 broadlooms till date. In 2018 we set up hand-tufting, and by 2021 we added our pitloom setup. Today we run a complete in-house workshop, start to finish.

    Started with 2 broadlooms.

  4. 2021
